## Patch surface
- NEW GboardRamblerSentenceLang (extension-src/.../rambler): multilingual (k2) AUTO dictation decides language per sentence; each sentence comes out all Bangla or all Latin. Falls back to the V29 path on any error.
- NEW GboardRamblerLoanSpell + generated GboardRamblerLoanTable: voice-only spelling for English words inside Bangla output. Order: override list (data/loanspell/overrides.tsv, 162 entries) -> CMU-derived table (data/loanspell/loan-table.tsv, 17,841 rows) -> V29 letter converter. Never touches words on the Bangla word lists. Background preload with no wait on the voice path.
- Voice exit text is NFC-normalized once; #hashtags and @handles stay Latin on every voice path.
- Smali hooks on the patch layer's own classes (ScriptFix, SegmentLock, Tier1): patches/v29.3-smali-hooks.diff. Typing and paste lanes are byte-identical to V29.
